**University of Dhaka** · *March 2023 – January 2024*

Designed the **Interval Creation Approach (ICA)**, a method for constructing interval type-2 fuzzy sets from a range of type-1 fuzzy set shapes — triangular, trapezoidal, Gaussian, generalized bell-shaped, symmetric and asymmetric. Benchmarked ICA against the Interval Approach, Enhanced Interval Approach, and M2 methods across synthetic and real-world datasets, reducing pre-processing time and overall computational cost.

This work led to a first-author paper at FUZZ-IEEE 2024. See [publications](/publications/).

*Tools:* Python, Java, C++, MATLAB, PyIT2FLS, Juzzy
