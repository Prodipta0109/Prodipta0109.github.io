## Overview

Investigated the comparative efficiency of type-1, general type-2, and interval type-2 fuzzy sets, and designed the **Interval Creation Approach (ICA)** — a methodology for constructing interval type-2 fuzzy sets from a range of type-1 fuzzy set shapes: triangular, trapezoidal, Gaussian, generalized bell-shaped, and both symmetric and asymmetric curves.
</div>

<div class="res-section" markdown="1">
## Approach

<div class="approach-grid" markdown="1">

<div class="approach-card" markdown="1">
<span class="step-num">1</span>

**Methodology design**

Designed the complete ICA methodology to construct interval type-2 fuzzy sets from the full range of type-1 fuzzy set shapes.
</div>

<div class="approach-card" markdown="1">
<span class="step-num">2</span>

**Evaluation planning**

Built a work-plan to demonstrate ICA's effectiveness using both synthetic and real-world datasets.
</div>

<div class="approach-card" markdown="1">
<span class="step-num">3</span>

**Benchmarking**

Performed a detailed comparison of ICA against the Interval Approach (IA), Enhanced Interval Approach (EIA), and M2 methods — the previously established approaches for this conversion.
</div>

<div class="approach-card" markdown="1">
<span class="step-num">4</span>

**Efficiency gains**

Reduced extra pre-processing time compared to prior methods, improving overall computational cost.
</div>
