## Backdoor Attacks in Large Language Model Reasoning

**Kennesaw State University, USA** · Supervisor: Dr. Honghui Xu · *July 2026 – Present*

Investigating how the reasoning process of large language models can be compromised. The work combines chain-of-thought analysis with trigger-injection techniques to identify where reasoning chains are vulnerable and how corrupted intermediate steps propagate to final answers.

*Tools:* Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers

## Security Analysis in Federated and Split Learning

**University of Maryland, Baltimore County, USA** · Supervisor: Dr. Md Tariqul Islam · *July 2025 – February 2026*

Studied the transferability of attacks and defenses between federated learning and split learning — two architectures that distribute training differently and therefore expose different attack surfaces. Covered model inversion, membership inference, and gradient leakage on the attack side, and differential privacy and secure aggregation on the defense side.

*Tools:* FedAvg, FedProx, split learning architectures, PyTorch, Python

**LUCID Lab, University of Nottingham, UK** · Supervisor: Dr. Shaily Kabir · *2024 – Present*

Identifying which input variables and fuzzy rules determine the robustness and reliability of a fuzzy inference system, and evaluating how non-singleton fuzzy sets behave as dataset noise increases.

## Interval Type-2 Fuzzy Sets: Behaviour and Performance

**University of Dhaka** · *March 2023 – January 2024*

Designed the **Interval Creation Approach (ICA)**, a method for constructing interval type-2 fuzzy sets from a range of type-1 fuzzy set shapes — triangular, trapezoidal, Gaussian, generalized bell-shaped, symmetric and asymmetric. Benchmarked ICA against the Interval Approach, Enhanced Interval Approach, and M2 methods across synthetic and real-world datasets, reducing pre-processing time and overall computational cost.

This work led to a first-author paper at FUZZ-IEEE 2024, part of the IEEE World Congress on Computational Intelligence. See [publications](/publications/).

*Tools:* Python, Java, C++, MATLAB, PyIT2FLS, Juzzy
