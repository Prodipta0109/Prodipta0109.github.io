  abstract  = {Movie recommendation systems have greatly advanced through AI and machine learning (ML), but challenges with recommendation accuracy still persist due to uncertain and noisy data. In this regard, singleton fuzzy logic systems (SFLSs) with crisp inputs and outputs offer simple and cost-effective solutions for ambiguous user preferences. However, SFLSs exhibit limitations in handling noisy and uncertain inputs -- such as caused by vague or incomplete/wrong text descriptions of movies and ratings, risking misleading recommendations. In this context, non-singleton FLSs (NSFLSs) that use fuzzy sets (FSs) to capture input uncertainties have the potential to enhance robustness and accuracy of the recommendation systems. This paper investigates an NSFLS-based movie recommendation system and presents a comparative analysis of its performance against an SFLS-based system. Experiments conducted on a real-world movie ratings data set with varying degrees of noise reveal NSFLSs as a useful approach to enhance the accuracy of movie recommendations with noisy data.}
