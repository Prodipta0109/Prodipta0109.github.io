An Android application that helps users find doctors and schedule appointments, maintaining dual profiles — one for medical professionals and one for patients — with different capabilities for each.

I contributed to the UX and UI design and the backend implementation, and prepared the requirement analysis and project documentation.

### Stack

Java, Firebase, Android Studio SDK, automated testing
